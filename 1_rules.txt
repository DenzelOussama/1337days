* **Tabs only** (tab width = 4 spaces), no spaces for indentation.
* **Maximum 25 lines** per function.
* **Maximum 5 variables** per function.
* **Maximum 4 named parameters** per function.
* **Maximum 5 functions** per `.c` file.
* **Declare variables at the top** of the function.
* **Leave one empty line** after declarations before the code.
* **One variable per line** (`int a; int b;`, not `int a, b;`).
* **No declaration and initialization together** (`int i; i = 0;`, not `int i = 0;`).
* **Maximum 80 characters** per line.
* **Use `snake_case`** for names.
* **Prefixes:** `s_` (struct), `t_` (typedef), `e_` (enum), `u_` (union), `g_` (global).
* **Forbidden:** `for`, `switch`, `case`, `goto`, `do...while`, ternary (`?:`), VLAs.
* **Code must compile** before the Norm matters.
* **Run `norminette`** on every file you write.
